Why RentEase

Built differently, for Rwanda.

The incumbents replicate Western listing sites without solving Rwanda-specific trust, payment, and language problems. We built differently.

Document Verification

Mandatory Titre Foncier upload and cross-verification with RLMUA. The only legally-anchored trust layer in Rwanda's rental market.

Secure Escrow Payments

Pay via MTN MoMo, Airtel Money, or card. Funds held in escrow and released to landlords only after you confirm you've moved in.

Digital Rental Agreements

Bilingual English/Kinyarwanda legally-compliant contracts, e-signed and stored securely. Eliminates handshake deals.

Transparent 10% Commission

One fair commission — 10% of the first month's rent — disclosed upfront. Never hidden. A fraction of what traditional brokers charge.
